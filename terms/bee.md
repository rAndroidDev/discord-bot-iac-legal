## What happens when you post

Your message is deleted immediately and sent for review. A moderator decides whether it is published. You are DM'd when a decision is made, and you can withdraw your submission before then using the button in that DM.

How your post is handled, and what is sent to Google's Gemini API during review, is described in the [privacy policy](../privacy/bee.md), which you can also reach by running `/privacy` in the server.
