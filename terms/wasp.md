## What Wasp does

Two things.

It removes accounts that post in a decoy channel set aside for catching automated and compromised accounts. That channel is clearly marked. Posting in it results in a kick and the removal of your recent messages. **A kick is not a ban**: if this happens to you and your account is yours again, you can rejoin.

It also screens the first few messages of new members for breaches of **rule 6** (soliciting, trading or buying Google Play testers) using Google's Gemini API, and raises anything it flags with a human moderator. A moderator decides what happens; the bot never warns, deletes or removes anything on its own judgement.

What message text is processed, how long the screening applies, and what is sent to Google is described in the [privacy policy](../privacy/wasp.md), which you can also reach by running `/privacy` in the server.

## Moderation decisions

A moderator makes every rule 6 decision. Automated flags are suggestions to a human, not findings.

If you believe a decision was wrong, contact the moderation team through the process in the server rules. Please do not argue moderation in public channels.

The automated kick from the decoy channel is the one action taken without a moderator reviewing it first. It is deliberately a kick rather than a ban so it is easy to undo by rejoining.
