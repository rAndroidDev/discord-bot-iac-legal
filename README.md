# /r/AndroidDev Discord bots

Privacy policies and terms of service for the bots that serve the
[/r/AndroidDev Discord community](https://discord.gg/D2cNrqX).

These applications are operated by the volunteer moderation team of that server. They are installed on this community’s Discord server and are not offered for other servers to add.

## Privacy policies

| Bot | Role | Policy |
|-----|------|--------|
| **Wasp** | Anti-spam and rule-6 (Play tester solicitation) moderation | [privacy/wasp.md](privacy/wasp.md) |
| **Bee** | Jobs channel review | [privacy/bee.md](privacy/bee.md) |
| **Mosquito** | April Fools | [privacy/mosquito.md](privacy/mosquito.md) |

Members can also reach their bot's policy by running `/privacy` in the server.
